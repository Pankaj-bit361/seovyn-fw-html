Storing an e-bike battery in winter

2026-09-15

Lithium-ion batteries don't like the cold, and they don't like sitting full for months.

Charge level

Store the battery at 30 to 60 percent, not empty and not full.

Temperature

Keep it between 50 and 70°F: indoors, not in a garage or shed.

Top it up

Check the charge every two or three months and top it up if it has dropped.
