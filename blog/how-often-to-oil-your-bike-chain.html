How often should you oil your bike chain?

2026-08-05

A clean, oiled chain shifts better and lasts longer. Too much oil is as bad as too little: it collects grit.

Clean it first

Wipe the chain with a rag while you pedal backwards. If it's very dirty, use a chain cleaner.

One drop per link

Put one drop of chain oil on each link, spin the cranks, then wipe off the excess.

How often

Every 100 to 200 miles, and straight after a ride in the rain.
